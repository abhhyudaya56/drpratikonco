Patient information

Frequently asked questions

General information to help you prepare for a consultation.

What should I bring to my first consultation?

Bring available biopsy or pathology reports, imaging reports and discs, blood test results, previous prescriptions, discharge summaries and a list of current medicines.

Do I need a referral to consult a surgical oncologist?

Referral requirements can vary. Contact the appointment desk to confirm the process and whether a referral is needed.

Does every cancer diagnosis require surgery?

No. Treatment depends on the cancer type, stage, location and the patient's overall health. Some patients may need surgery, other treatments, or a combination. The treating team can explain suitable options.

Can I book a consultation to get a second opinion?

You can contact the appointment desk to ask about consultation availability for a second opinion. Bring your existing reports and treatment records.
